# ProxyForGame Calculators

This directory contains documentation for all OGame calculators.

Generated by `scripts/generate-docs.js` - run `make docs` rather than editing by hand.

## Available Calculators

### [OGame - Costs calculator](costs.md)

Calculate the cost and build time of buildings, research, ships, and defenses in OGame, including universe speed and bonuses.

**View:** [OGame - Costs calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/costs.php)

### [OGame - Expeditions calculator](expeditions.md)

Estimate the resources, ships, and Dark Matter you may find on OGame expeditions and compare expected returns for different fleets.

**View:** [OGame - Expeditions calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/expeditions.php)

### [OGame - Flight time and savepoints calculator](flight.md)

Calculate fleet flight time, deuterium consumption, and the best departure time in OGame, based on universe speed, ships, and flight mode.

**View:** [OGame - Flight time and savepoints calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/flight.php)

### [OGame - Graviton calculator](graviton.md)

Find how many Solar Satellites and how much energy you need to research Graviton Technology in OGame, based on your planet settings.

**View:** [OGame - Graviton calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/graviton.php)

### [OGame - Costs calculator for LifeForms](lfcosts.md)

Calculate the cost and build time of Lifeform buildings and research in OGame, including race and technology bonuses.

**View:** [OGame - Costs calculator for LifeForms](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/lfcosts.php)

### [OGame - Moons calculator](moon.md)

Calculate your chance of finding a moon, the outcome of a moon destruction attempt, and Sensor Phalanx range in OGame.

**View:** [OGame - Moons calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/moon.php)

### [OGame - Production speed calculator](production.md)

Calculate metal, crystal, and deuterium production, energy use, and mine upgrade payback for one planet or your entire OGame empire.

**View:** [OGame - Production speed calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/production.php)

### [OGame - Construction queue](queue.md)

Compare the cost and time of building upgrades in sequence on an OGame planet or moon, including factory levels and universe speed.

**View:** [OGame - Construction queue](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/queue.php)

### [OGame - Terraformer calculator](terraformer.md)

Calculate the energy, resources, and time needed to terraform an OGame planet, including Solar Satellites and bonuses.

**View:** [OGame - Terraformer calculator](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/terraformer.php)

### [OGame - Trade calculator - Resource exchange](trade.md)

Compare metal, crystal, and deuterium trades in OGame and see how much you receive at a given exchange rate.

**View:** [OGame - Trade calculator - Resource exchange](http://pfg.wmp/ogame/calc/trade.php)
